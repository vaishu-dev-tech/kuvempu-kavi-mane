About Kuvempu

Kuvempu (Kuppalli Venkatappa Puttappa) was born on 29 December 1904 in Kuppalli, Karnataka. He grew up surrounded by nature, which deeply influenced his thoughts and literary works.

He became the first Kannada writer to receive the prestigious Jnanpith Award, one of India's highest literary honors, for his epic work "Sri Ramayana Darshanam".

Kuvempu believed in “Vishwa Manava” (Universal Humanism), promoting equality, unity, and respect for all humans beyond caste and religion.

Some of his most famous works include "Malegalalli Madumagalu", "Kanuru Heggadithi", and many poems that reflect nature and philosophy.

He served as the Vice-Chancellor of the University of Mysore, contributing significantly to education and culture.

Kuvempu passed away on 11 November 1994, but his ideas and writings continue to inspire generations across Karnataka and beyond.
