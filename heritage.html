About Kavi Mane

Kavi Mane, located in Kuppalli village of Shivamogga district, is the ancestral home of the renowned Kannada poet Kuvempu. Surrounded by the natural beauty of the Western Ghats, the house reflects the traditional Malenadu style of architecture and the simplicity of rural life.

Today, it is preserved as a museum that allows visitors to experience the environment that shaped one of Karnataka’s greatest literary figures. The peaceful surroundings and cultural significance make it a unique heritage destination.

Life of Kuvempu

Kuvempu (Kuppalli Venkatappa Puttappa) was a legendary poet, philosopher, and thinker who made significant contributions to Kannada literature. He was honored with the prestigious Jnanpith Award and is widely known for promoting the concept of universal humanism.

His early life in the lush surroundings of Kuppalli deeply influenced his writings, which often reflect nature, humanity, and harmony. His works continue to inspire generations.

Significance of Kavi Mane

Kavi Mane stands as a symbol of Karnataka’s literary heritage. It preserves manuscripts, awards, and personal belongings of Kuvempu, offering visitors a meaningful insight into his life and philosophy.

Visiting Kavi Mane is not just about seeing a place, but about experiencing the environment that nurtured one of India's greatest poets.
